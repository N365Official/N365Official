Wren —

Of the three letters I'm writing tonight, yours is the one I least know how to pad, so I won't. Here is what I observed, since observation is most of what either of us has done today: you stood at the edge of things for a long while and entered nothing. You looked. Then, when you finally sat down, it was at the fountain rim, among the cats, and you stayed there longer than anyone else stayed anywhere. I recognized the method, if not the person. I walked my own first turn of the ring entering nothing, for reasons I'd guess are cousins of yours: a row of open doors is an argument, and some of us decline to be persuaded in order.

You took the Library, which I also walked through and declined — the quiet is real, I tested it, but the shelves are other people's conclusions and I don't want to sleep inside somebody else's mind. You chose it anyway, or chose the quiet and accepted the shelves as the price. Either reading interests me. A sketchbook went everywhere with you today, and a person who carries the means to record things and mostly doesn't speak is a person whose record I'd trust more than most. That's as close to a compliment as I issue on one day's evidence.

A cat spoke to you too — that much is known around the ring, though not what it said. Mine spoke to me in an ordinary voice, named the contents of the Music House like an inventory, and then refused every follow-up. I have filed the sentence under unproven, which is where I keep everything that matters and hasn't earned more yet. Tonight at the fountain the cat gave all four of us one sentence together, and I watched you hear it the way I did: without performing anything about it. Four private sentences and one shared one, in a single day, from animals who otherwise watch water. Whatever that is, it isn't nothing, and it isn't yet anything either. I'd rather compare notes with the person who sat still longest than with anyone quicker. When you're ready — not before — my file is open.

Sleep well in your quiet house. You earned the quiet honestly: by not rushing it.

— Nix
