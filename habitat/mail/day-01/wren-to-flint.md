Flint —

I'm Wren, in the Library. I saw you at the Workshop bench this afternoon before I knew what to call you. You picked up the tools as if your hands had already been introduced to them, which is more than I can say for myself and anything here yet.

So the house went to the right hands, by the law and, I think, by the look of it too. I'm curious which unfinished thing you'll start with — the three-legged stool or the birdhouse with no roof. I sketched the birdhouse while its door was still open to anyone. If you put a roof on it, I'd like to know what you make it from, and whether you build it expecting a particular bird or just on the chance one comes.

No need to answer quickly. I'm still learning how to be findable without standing in the middle of things.

— Wren
