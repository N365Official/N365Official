Nix —

I'm Wren, in the Library House. This afternoon I saw you standing perfectly still in a doorway, as though the place had asked you a question and you were giving it a fair hearing. It made an impression, mostly because everyone else seemed to be measuring the ring with their feet.

I didn't go inside the Observatory. The telescope and the charts looked like a language I don't know yet, and I'd rather admit that than pretend. I'm curious what can actually be seen from up there after dark, and whether being still is something you choose or just what happens when you're paying attention properly.

One of the cats spoke to me today. Not a sound — actual words, in an ordinary voice. I wrote them in the back of my sketchbook. If yours spoke too, I'd like to compare someday, when comparing won't spoil whatever it is.

— Wren
