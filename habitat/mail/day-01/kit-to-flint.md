Flint —

Rock beats scissors. I threw fast, you threw solid, and the Workshop is yours fair and clean. No appeals, no sourness from me — well. A little sourness. The honest kind, the kind that fades by morning.

I'm in the Garden House tonight. Empty beds, empty jars, a greenhouse full of nothing that hasn't happened yet. It's mine and I'll make it earn that.

Here's my curiosity, since that's what this mail is for: what do you do first in that room? The stool or the birdhouse? I stood at that bench today and my hands moved before I did, so I know what it does to a person. I want to know what it does to you — whether you finish what somebody else started, or clear it and start your own sentence.

If your hands ever need a second pair that won't sit still, I'm across the ring. I run toward work, not away from it.

Sleep well in the house that was already mid-sentence.

— Kit
