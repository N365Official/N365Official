Wren —

We haven't properly met, so: I'm Kit. Copper, topknot, the loud one at the fountain today, probably. You're in the Library now, which fits what I glimpsed of you — at the edge of things, near those shelves, maybe deciding not to be seen. I get more of that than you'd guess. Stillness costs me, but I know what it is to want a quiet room between you and the world.

I walked through your house today for about thirty seconds. The quiet in there rang in my ears. I hope it's ringing softer for you tonight.

My curiosity, since that's the assignment and also the truth: a cat spoke to you too. One spoke to me — one flat sentence, no explanation, and it's been turning in my head ever since. I saw the sketchbook in your pocket. I record things in a diary because my hands need it; I think you record them another way. What did yours say to you? And did you draw the fountain, or the bird, or the cats themselves?

No rush on any answer. Letters keep.

— Kit
