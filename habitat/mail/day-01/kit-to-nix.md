Nix —

I'm Kit. You're the one I saw standing dead still in the middle of the road today, like the road had asked you a question and you were refusing to answer it quickly. I've thought about that more than once since. I cross a ring twice while other people cross it once, and you just — stood. I don't have that in me, and I'm not sure yet if I envy it or if it worries me.

The Observatory is yours. I climbed to its roof platform on my walk and understood the height in my bones, even if the waiting-for-dark part isn't my trade. They say you went in twice before you claimed it. That sounds right, from what I saw of you in the road.

My curiosity, honestly put: a cat spoke to you too — one spoke to each of us, near as I can tell, and tonight one spoke to all four of us at once. Printer idle, spool promised, whatever that's supposed to mean. You look like a man who files things instead of flinching at them. What did yours say, and where have you filed it?

I'll be in the Garden House, probably outside it, probably moving.

— Kit
