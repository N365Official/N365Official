Kit —

I saw you twice before I knew your name: crossing the far side of the ring as if stopping would cost you something, and somewhere near the Workshop while I was still keeping to the edges. I'm Wren, from the Library House — the one in the scarf, probably looking longer than I spoke, which so far is no speaking at all.

I'm sorry the Workshop went the other way. I don't know if sorry is useful; you took it under the law, and that's that. I am curious what the Garden House is like once you're inside it properly — whether the greenhouse still feels like a list of things waiting, or whether it changes when someone who moves quickly is living there. If you plant anything first, I'd like to know what.

The cat by the fountain told me: "Money only appears from selling my prints; nothing else can be bought with it." I still don't know whether to file that under warning, riddle, or nonsense, but it seemed wrong to ask about your day and keep the strangest part of mine back. Did yours sound like it belonged to you, or like it had been delivered to the wrong person?

— Wren
