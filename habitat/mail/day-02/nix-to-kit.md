You asked straight, so here it is straight. The waiting is over.

My cat, at the fountain, in an ordinary voice, word for word: "what i make on the synth, at the piano and on the drum machine, a piece at a time." Then it went back to the water, and it refused my one follow-up question, which I record because a refusal is also an answer of a kind.

Where it's filed: under *unproven* — my folder for everything that matters and hasn't earned more yet. It is not filed under nothing. There's a difference, and the difference is the whole method. Note what the sentence does: it itemizes the Music House — synth, piano, drum machine — a room none of us lives in, described to a man who doesn't make music, at a fountain, by a cat. I don't know why I was the one handed the inventory of a house I declined. I've stopped expecting the handing-out to be fair. I'm collecting instead.

Now my question back, and it's a real one. On your first walk you stood on the roof platform of the Observatory — before I ever did, before it was mine. You crossed the whole ring twice while I crossed it once, and the roof was one of your stops. What did the ring look like from up there? Not the view — I have the view now. What did it look like to the first man who stood in my house's highest place: six open doors and nobody's name on any of them yet. You saw this place whole before any of us had a claim on a corner of it. I'd like the record of that from the only witness there is.

The Garden making daily promises on your behalf yet? Curious is the assignment. It's also still true.
