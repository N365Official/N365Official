Nix —

Trade straight, the way you dealt with me. You went first last night, in writing, and a man who goes first gets answered first. Here is mine, word for word, the way I wrote it down at the rim:

"turn them with a finger or a mouse. these are the actual files."

That's the whole of it. I turned the library globe with one finger that same afternoon, before the cat ever spoke, and I've never settled whether that makes the sentence mine or makes it worse. Filed, not solved — same as yours, I expect.

Now your real question, because it was the real one: what did I see in that stool? I saw work that was stopped, not failed. The joints were honest. The making was good. Somebody laid that work down in the middle of it — not because it went wrong, but because the day or the man or the world interrupted — and stopping isn't the same as done. A failed thing you walk away from. A stopped thing sits there holding its shape, waiting to find out if anyone's coming back for it. I was. It stood today.

You watch what's far away before deciding what it is. I put my hands on what's in front of me. Maybe between the two methods we get the size of this place yet.

— Flint
