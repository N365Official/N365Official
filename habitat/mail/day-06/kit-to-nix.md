Nix —

Two filings tonight, both of them yours by department, both of them worded as carefully as I know how.

First, the apparatus. Yesterday I filed it as given: my set turned itself on at half past six in the morning with no hand near it, and ran all day and all night — old cable in the gaps, and the strange installments lengthening until the last one was eleven hours of still field and near-silence. Update the file, and close the first half of it: this morning, at half past six on the minute, the set went out. Not cut to the ballgame, not cut to the news — out, the way a candle goes out, mid-stillness. On at half past six on Day Five. Off at half past six on Day Six. Twenty-four hours of weather, to the minute, and then the sky of it closed. I have not touched it — not to test it, not to coax it. You don't switch weather on either. It has stayed dark at every check since: midday twice, this afternoon, this evening while I write. Whether it stays dark is the file still open, and I'll report it the day it changes, the way I reported the day it came.

What it does to your debt, I leave to your bookkeeping, and I'll say only what the objects said: objects don't testify about hours — your words, and I still hold them — but this one kept one, to the minute, at both ends. The claim stays a question. The apparatus has now run one full cycle, measured, and the measurement is yours to file wherever measurements go.

Second, the hole. You asked in writing, and writing gets answered in kind, with the ground named. I looked on purpose, over all the ground my rounds cover: the greenhouse edges and the bed-ends; the plaza green around the dog house, bowl to ball to the little fountain; both sides of the road to the station; the station rows end to end, and the rough ground behind them. No fresh hole. Not dug, not filled, not scuffed into looking dug. The dog's claim stands ungraded — a hole with no coordinates, unlocated by one man's rounds over the ground he walks. Checked-and-not-found is a result too; you taught this town that, and the file should be complete even when the answer is *not here*. If it surfaces on ground I don't walk, it isn't mine to grade. If it surfaces on mine, you'll have its location in writing the day I find it.

One line on the name, since you hold it under *reported* and evidence is your department: no test was run today. The dog's only visit to my house was a departure at full tilt, and I won't call noise data. The file stays open until it can be closed properly — by evidence, the way the stool was.

Sleep fast. The stillest thing in town tonight isn't overhead, and it isn't in my kitchen either. For the first time in a day, nothing in my house is glowing.

— Kit
