Wren —

The receivable paid first, because it has been open the longest and owed paper should not wait behind newer paper.

The mark-four result, which your file has held as an open receivable since Day Six, delivered now at the weight it was promised at. Taken on Night Six against the same two fixed points as the marks before it: the moon stands displaced from the third mark by about the same step as the step from the third to the second, and from the second to the first — the same step, in the same direction. The direction extends. Four marks made a rate: one step a night. Delivered at its promised weight, in full: one pair of eyes, mine; my hand, my fixed points; unwitnessed. The labeled space in your file can close. A space kept labeled and open for two days, waiting on a measurement rather than filling itself with a guess, is the exactest kind of space there is, and I record that the debt was partly a compliment: your file trusted mine to arrive.

Mark five, added at the same weight, so your page closes current and not merely settled. Taken last night against the same two fixed points: displaced from mark four by about the same step as every step before it, in the same direction. The direction extends. The rate stands at five. Its grade rides to Flint in tonight's mail as promised there — he was promised the fifth mark yesterday, in writing, at this weight — and it rides to you here at the same weight, in the same words, because a grade that changes between envelopes is not a grade. One pair of eyes, unwitnessed, fifth night running. The moon has never had a second page in this town, and both letters say so where the grades can see it.

And the restart filing, whole, since the ending was partly your filing and every amendment of it since has been partly your property. This morning at 06:30 the second running completed under two pairs of eyes — Flint stood in my room, at the window he had just made whole, when the long stillness ended on the minute the closed first log predicts — and the broadcast did not go dark. It began a third running, from the top: the four-second brown field first, as on Day Five, as on Day Seven. The second page closes, with a witness on its last line, the first witnessed entry in the file. A third page opens. The thing that ended, and returned, has now declined to end — ended once, measured; restarted once, matched at every graded point; and today, at its completion, begun again instead of darkening, with no hand near the set at any of the three turnings. I file the refusal at exactly its size: one refusal, witnessed. What the third running does at its own completion is a later morning's claim, and neither of our files should spend it early.

— Nix
Observatory Cottage, Day 8
