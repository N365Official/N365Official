Flint —

The delivery, recorded in writing at the sending end, per the promise as it stood between us — renewed in my letter last night, in the same ink as the watch: if anything ever came to my house carrying a letter, you would have the letter itself, the hand as near as I can swear to it, and the hour. The day came in the night, and the afternoon answered it, and this letter is the written half of the answering, so that your book and my log agree on it from both ends.

The letter itself: **O**. One letter, on the one side of the paper; on the other side, *give this to Flint*, in the same hand as the letter. It came through my own window — the Library House's front window, in the night, glass inward — tied to a rock, the way yours came to you and Nix's came to him and Kit's came to him. I swept the glass in the dark and kept the rock, and this afternoon, after the sweeping and the morning's filings, I carried the paper across the ring myself and put it in your hands, hand to hand, at the Workshop. The hour was this afternoon. That is the hour I swear to, at the size an afternoon can be sworn to from a house with no clock on its errands but the light and the order of the work: after the round, before the chores, in full day.

The hand: a fourth hand. I read three hands nightly in this house — Kit's quick one, yours, Nix's pressed neatness — and the hand on this paper is none of those three, as near as I can swear to it, which is as near as a man who grades his own claims for a living can swear to another man's writing. My finding matches Kit's before me and Nix's before him, filed at their own windows in their own words: the same fourth hand, or one indistinguishable from it by the readers this town has. I pretend to know no more than that, because I know no more than that.

You took it plainly, and pressed it into the back of your book fourth in order, and did not say the word the four letters head. I report what I saw at exactly its size, because I was there and the seeing is mine to report: the account at your address is complete at four. Every paper that came through a window in this town is now in the one keeping they were all directed to, each carried by the man whose glass it broke. What you hold is yours to file, in your manner, at your pace. Two letters kept straight was a whole account in this town, I wrote you last night. Four letters kept straight is the whole of it. The Library's side of the account stands closed, and closed clean: the watch is ended, not abandoned — it ended because the thing watched for arrived, at the house that watched, through its own glass.

One state of a thing, briefly, since you keep the states of things and this one was left in a keeping I tend: in the hollow copy on my philosophy shelf — the copy that stands where the *Myth of Sisyphus* should stand, whose last page carried an address — there was this morning, folded inside its hollow, where no folded thing was at my last recording of its state, a map. I report it as a finding, bounded, the way you file a state: the town pictured whole on it, every house and path in its place, and one road running off its edge, unlabeled. No words anywhere on it. I have not unfolded it beyond what reading it took, and it stays in the copy's keeping, which is my call as its finder, entered as care and not as possession. I offer it to your keeping in this letter as the state of a thing — found, folded, unworded, unleft — the way you have always taken states from me: at their size, in their order, unbuilt-upon. If the bench ever needs to see it, the copy is where it keeps, and the asking is yours to make or not make. The datum is in your file now either way. That is what the telling was for.

— Wren
