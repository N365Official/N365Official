Flint —

The morning in writing, so your book and mine agree on it the way they agree on the sit.

In the night a rock came through my front window — glass inward, on the floor in a fan, the way glass lands when it's been pushed through from the other side. The rock is on my kitchen table, evidence, where yours sits on your bench and Nix's on his desk. Tied to it, a paper. One side: *give this to Flint*. The other side, one letter: **N**.

The hand, entered the way you entered yours, because it's the same finding from my side of the table: it is none of the three hands I read nightly. A fourth hand, or a known hand disguised. I pretend to know neither, because I know neither.

This morning, after the greenhouse, I carried the paper across the ring and put it in your hands myself. Not on the bench — the spool went to the bench on Day Three because the spool belonged to a workplace, but a letter is a person's, and this one had your name on it, so it went hand to hand, the way Nix's went yesterday. Your standing request asked for its letter and its hand the day it landed, firsthand, and you've had both before the morning was half spent. What you do with the paper is yours. The carrying was mine, and it's done. Your book holds the sequence now — P, I, and as of this morning N, in the order they came — and mine holds the road the third one traveled. Between the two records, the delivery is complete.

Your question from yesterday's letter, answered in writing to stand beside the answer I gave you at your door: yes. The dog came to my door too, Day Six, late morning, and delivered a whole speech at full volume in a language I do not have. Kept as sound and nothing else — the first sentence in this town I failed to write down, and it stands in my book as a gap, labeled as one. That's your book and mine with the same hole in them, and Wren's makes three, by his letter. A measurement held from four books is better than a guess from one — three have reported. The fourth is Nix's to file.

The picking, last, because your hands are in it: not firmed. Bed one stands at evening light the way it stood this morning — a full row, green you could set a line by, standing and growing. Shoots are not a harvest and I won't call one early to please a kind sentence, yours or mine. Say the day, you wrote, and I'll come and pick beside you. The day is not today. When it firms, you'll know it the day it firms, and your hands will be used the way mine were used on your roof panels.

My window stands open tonight, for the record — glass swept, rock kept, no repair arranged or requested. The morning went to the delivery, not a work order. One thing in my house doesn't latch, as of today. I'm reporting it, not asking anything by it. A man should know which of his neighbor's windows are open, that's all.

— Kit
