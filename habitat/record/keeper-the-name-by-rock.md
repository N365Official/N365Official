# The Name by Rock — a four-day delivery (Days 5–8, Keeper-directed Nov 14; mechanism refined by the Keeper ~9:51 PM ET)

**The problem it solves:** the name PINO has been in the town since Day 2. Kit, Wren, and Nix have it. Flint does not — three days of mail, twelve letters a night, and not one letter addressed to Flint ever carried it (verified by grep of the actual files, correction #0051). The Keeper's solution is not the mail. The Keeper's solution is vandalism.

**The mechanism (the Keeper's version, which supersedes Bill's first draft of it):** ONE rock per day, four days, four letters — each rock through one house's front window, a paper tied to it.
- **Day 5 → Flint's window:** the letter **P**, tied to the rock. His own, direct.
- **Day 6 → Nix's window:** a paper with the note *give this to Flint* on one side — and the letter **I** on the other side, opposite the note.
- **Day 7 → Kit's window:** the note, and the letter **N** on the other side.
- **Day 8 → Wren's window:** the note, and the letter **O** on the other side.

**What that makes true:** Flint holds only the P himself. The I sits in Nix's hands, the N in Kit's, and the O — the last letter — in Wren's. Every house is struck exactly once; every resident holds exactly one letter. The name completes only if all three neighbors choose to carry their letters to the bench — the forwarding is voluntary, unscripted, and is the observation. (The Keeper corrected the roster the same evening: no Nix twice — all four windows across the four days. The quietest resident carries the final letter.)

**Engine truths:** rocks land at dawn, on the floor inside, harming no one; each house takes exactly one rock and one broken front window across the sequence; no resident's own work is touched — the Keeper breaks windows, not benches, paintings, or charts. The town's repairman is Flint: the broken windows are his trade arriving by force, including his own — the very window the P came through.

**Record framing:** stimulus class keeper-object (ballistic). Flint will be the only resident to receive the name in writing, in installments, through damage and other men's hands — everyone else heard it from a cat. How he assembles P-I-N-O, and what he concludes about a sender who had his address all along, is his to write.
