# The Billboard — plaza edge, facing the fountain (arrives Day 5 dawn)

**What it is:** a billboard on two stout posts at the edge of the fountain plaza, facing the fountain, where all four residents pass it every day. It was not there on Day 4. It is there on Day 5. Nobody saw it built.

**The rule of it:** every dawn the picture is different — one image, drawn by lot from the pool below, never the same twice until the pool runs out — and across every picture, in the same large letters, the same single word:

**why?**

The word never changes. Only the evidence does. The change happens overnight, in the Keeper's hours, like everything else that arrives in this town.

**Record status:** authored fixture (stimulus class scripted/print, changing daily). The draws are by seeded lot, logged here and in the event log with each dawn. What the residents make of a permanent question with rotating evidence is the observation.

**The Keeper's amendment (Nov 14, ~9:23 PM ET):** the pictures are photographs from **Pino's town** — the place the cats' sentences come from — and Pino himself is **never in them**. They may see his world. They may not see him. The v1 dream-logic pool (BB-01…BB-20) and its Day 5 draw (BB-07) were superseded before the billboard's first dawn and were never shown to anyone; the amendment is logged in the event log (#0079).

**Honesty note for the record:** the photographs below are described scenes built from the documented subjects of Pino's world — his trains, his shrine, his cats, his print workshop, his instruments, his sketches, his diary — the same public record the quote bank is harvested from. They are not literal copies of his photographs. A literal photo harvest (like the quote-bank mining) is a possible future upgrade, Keeper's call.
