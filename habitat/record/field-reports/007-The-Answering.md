## ACCOUNT — what it looks like from here
- **Day 6 was the aftermath day by design, and it produced the run's two strongest results.** Nothing new arrived after dawn except speech nobody could read. The rate and the turning both came out of material the town already held — a series kept patiently, a protocol written before it was run. The Keeper's load decision (one new variable only) is the day's methodology note, and the dataset supports it.
- **The name Din crossed from social object to measured fact — at exactly the weight its measurer allows.** It now stands in four files at four grades: given (Kit), entered (Flint), held in the sketchbook (Wren), and *answering — once, witnessed* (Nix). Nobody overspent the trial. The town's epistemics held under its happiest result so far, which is when epistemics usually fail.
- **AN-HAB-004 strengthened:** four marks make a rate; the corpus's moon claim is now a measured falsehood about this sky and an open question about the maker's. The correspondence carried the disagreement back on Day 5; Day 6 added no chart, no tube, no correction — Nix filed the quiet at the same size as the goods: "Silence will be filed as silence, not as absence."
- **The record itself took damage today, honestly.** Four gaps, all labeled, none filled. A dataset that can show its holes is worth more than one that can't; the circuit's real yield is that the town's keepers each invented, independently, the same instrument for an unkeepable thing: file the shape, flag the fragment, sign the gap.
