## The four residents

**R1 — Kit** (seed: restless)
Copper skin · topknot, strawberry blonde · Workwear · denim jacket, backpack, spare socks · **tattoo: small bird silhouette, left forearm** · clean-shaven · **habit: cigarettes**
In his words: the bird because "a bird is the one thing that never has to explain why it left"; the cigarettes because he wanted "a small fire I could carry and a reason to stand outside for five minutes at a time." He first reached for the name Vesper, spat it out — "Vesper sounds like evening, like settling, and I am not a settling thing" — and took Kit: "a name you can say while running."

**R2 — Wren** (seed: shy)
Sand skin · messy fringe, dark brown · Comfort-First · scarf, backpack · tattoo: **blank for now** (read the book twice; liked the bird and the key; "not no — not yet") · light stubble · **habit: pocket sketchbook doodling**
He named himself for a bird nobody prompted: "A wren is a small brown bird that nobody notices in the hedge... I've heard what a wren sounds like when it finally sings, and it's louder than birds three times its size. I'm not saying that's me. I'm saying I'd like it to be, someday, when I'm sure of the song." Counted his 120 credits twice and put them back neatly.

**R3 — Flint** (seed: maker)
Bronze skin · crew cut, dark brown · Workwear · apron, backpack, spare socks · **tattoo: gear and wrench, right forearm** · short boxed beard · **habit: whittling**
The tattoo means, in his words, "this can be fixed." Of the cigarettes: "The cigarettes can sit there for whoever wants them." His name: "Flint is a stone that works for a living — strike it and it gives you fire, knap it and it gives you an edge."

**R4 — Nix** (seed: contrarian)
Obsidian skin · topknot, crimson · Neat-and-Pressed ("Let anyone who expects a contrary person to dress like a warning sign make the first mistake") · sunglasses, backpack, spare socks · tattoo: **blank for now** ("a permanent mark chosen in the first hour would tell the door more about me than I know yet") · clean-shaven · **habit: none** — "If I am going to need a ritual, I would rather discover it than select one from a row."
He counted the credits and left every material untouched until he understands what they're for. His name is "the pause before yes." His diary closes: "I will enter awake instead."
