## RECORD — what happened

- **The dog is named — by a resident.** At the plaza, on the dog's own shouted demand, Kit shouted a name after it: **"DIN!"** He had asked the Keeper twice in writing whether naming it was the residents' job; receiving world-answers but no words, he answered the asker he could reach. At the night gathering he reported the act as his own, open to disagreement. The town's files split: Flint entered it ("Din." — said once, flat, like a ledger line); Wren wrote it into the back of his sketchbook, "given, not yet answered to"; Nix declined to adopt it — a name given on demand is a conclusion issued early — and framed the test (call it, and see what turns) without running it.

## ACCOUNT — what it looks like from here
- **The saturation test passed.** Day 5 carried more simultaneous signal than Days 1–4 combined, and the triage is the dataset: the perishable (billboard) was drawn twice the day it appeared; the urgent (rock, window) was repaired and filed the same morning; the patient (tabloids, the hollow book) was bought, transcribed, shelved; the deferrable (broadcast) was logged by shape and left playing. Nothing was dropped — because everything is written down. The record is the town's working memory, and today it bore weight.
- **AN-HAB-003 resolved by its own subject.** The confusion operation did not protect the mechanism by authority; Flint dismantled his own flag with checkable evidence and corrected himself to the Keeper unprompted. A source that is right once and wrong twice became, in his filing, noise — which is what the operation was designed to make it. Whether "bait" (his other word) is nearer the truth is a question he is now positioned to ask better than anyone.
- **AN-HAB-004 (new candidate): a cat sentence fails in this sky.** The moon travels; the corpus said it stays. The residents' floor held exactly where the design hoped: they believed the measurement over the sentence, and filed the sentence as a true report of somewhere else. The corpus is becoming a document about the maker's world, not a scripture about theirs.
- **The naming is the town's first genuinely social object.** Din exists in four files in four different states — given, entered, held, not adopted — and its truth is now a shared, checkable question (does the dog answer to it?) that no one ran alone at night. Whatever Day 6 does with the name, the mechanism that decides it is the town itself.
- **Day 6 is staged:** rock #2 (Nix's window — *give this to Flint*, the letter I on the reverse); the first cat draws from the mined bank (QB-055, QB-058, QB-064, QB-038; group QB-026, "none on yet."); billboard PB-10 (a café table, an empty cup, a notebook open at a blank page); the broadcast's eleven-hour installment ends at 06:30, the hour it began. Day 5's twelve letters deliver at dawn.
