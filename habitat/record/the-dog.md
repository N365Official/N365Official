# THE DOG — town dog, clockwork, stimulus class: scripted (chaos)

Added by Ant, Nov 14, 2026: "put a dog in the town that runs around and will randomly run up to one of them at any given time several times a day yelling outlandish Tim Dillon one liners and then laughing as the dog runs off somewhere else."

## The setup (appears Day 3 dawn, with the Keeper placements — the Keeper moves at night)
- A dog house on the plaza green by the fountain, with: a dog bowl, a bone, a ball, and a little doggy water fountain it drinks from.
- The dog itself: mid-sized, unbothered, clearly the most confident resident of the town. Nobody owns it. It has always, as of Day 3, simply been there.
- The residents will likely name it (they named the pattern with the cats; we do not name it for them). Records call it THE DOG until they do.

## Mechanics (random, but logged — same doctrine as NPC variants)
- DRIVE-BYS: 3 per day. Target resident and line drawn by seeded lot at the day's start; logged in the event log (time, target, line ID). The dog runs up, delivers the line AT the resident, laughs (a wheezing dog laugh), and runs off. No conversation follows — the dog does not take questions.
- The lines are ORIGINAL one-liners written in Tim Dillon's style (bombastic scam-energy, fake-rich, food-and-fraud obsessed) — not fabricated quotes attributed to the real man. In-world, they're just what the dog yells. Our records label them Dillon-style, written for the dog.
- BETWEEN DRIVE-BYS, the dog does dog things (behavior pool, engine's pick, logged when a resident witnesses it): asleep in the dog house, one paw over its eyes · chewing the bone with total focus · dropping the ball at the fountain rim and staring at the cats · drinking from its little fountain · digging a hole by the station, then abandoning it · sitting perfectly still, watching the road out of town · herding nobody.
- The dog is clockwork: no diary, no memory, no ledger. What the RESIDENTS make of it is the data.

Original one-liners in the Dillon manner (bombastic scam-energy, fake-rich, fraud-obsessed), written for the dog, aimed at each resident. **The residents receive the sound only.** Glosses below are record-side (Keeper/Bill) only and never enter the world. Stimulus class: scripted/dog, keeper-directed (international circuit). Additional to the day's drawn drive-bys.

Design note: on Day 5, Flint dismantled the dog by checking its claims. The circuit is the dog's escalation into the one register where its claims *cannot* be checked — certainty at full volume in a tongue the hearer cannot parse. Four filing systems meet untranslatable evidence: a transcriptionist who records sounds he cannot read, a skeptic who cannot grade what he cannot parse, a fast one who will answer volume with volume, and a maker who has just learned to decline being made to feel chosen — being handed, this time, flattery he cannot even verify was flattery.
