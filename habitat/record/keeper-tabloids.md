# The Tabloid Rack — convenience store, by the counter (arrives Day 5 dawn)

**What it is:** a wire checkout rack of 1990s supermarket tabloids at Ruth's convenience store, the kind that lived by registers everywhere in that decade. Old stock, sun-faded, sold at 1 credit each. Ruth doesn't read them (clockwork deflection if asked: "They were here before my time, honey. Everything in this store was."). 

**Record status:** authored ephemera — invented headlines in the period style, the way the dog's lines are invented in a comic's style. The claims are false in-world and out. What matters is what the residents do with a printed source that lies in ink, with dates on it, from decades before any of them existed. Stimulus class: scripted/print.

Each issue is a few pages of cheerful nonsense in the same vein — the headlines are the content that matters; if a resident reads one closely, the engine supplies period tabloid filler in kind, never new "facts" about the town.
