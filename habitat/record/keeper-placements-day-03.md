# Keeper Placements — Day 3 dawn (RULED by Ant, Nov 14, 2026)

Ant's ruling: the Keeper answers the four letters THROUGH THE WORLD — things left for them to find, NO WORDS. The Phase 1 silence holds in language; the world itself responds. All placements appear overnight, found at Day 3 dawn — the same pattern as the postbox (the Keeper moves at night; they will notice). Logged as stimulus class: keeper-object. Nothing placed alters any resident's own work.

1. THE SPOOL (fountain rim — communal; answers Kit's + Nix's spool questions, and the group quote's promise)
   A new spool of filament, sealed, sitting on the fountain rim at dawn — where the cat stood when it said "new spool promised." No note, no name. The promise was kept; by whom, the delivery is the only signature; to whom — undecided. Whoever takes it, takes it (a contest would go to RPS under the law).

2. THE UNFINISHED BIRD (Flint's bench — answers "was the stool left unfinished on purpose?" + his offer of work)
   A small carved wooden bird, body finished smooth, the head still a rough block, left on his bench overnight. The answer in kind: yes — things here are left unfinished on purpose; finishing is the residents' part. It also lands inside his live question — the birdhouse roof, and whether to expect a particular bird.

3. THE FIELD GUIDE (Library, natural-history shelves — answers Wren's "is the bird Pino?")
   A field guide to birds among the natural-history shelves he mapped on Day 2, fallen open at the kiwi: a small, flightless, nocturnal bird. Information, not revelation — the world honestly contains it, and his search honestly finds it. It gives him the scale clue in print: the statue is a monument; Pino is small. It does not say where Pino is.

4. THE CHART TUBE (Observatory, by the telescope — answers Nix's "whose hand marked the charts?")
   A chart tube that was not there yesterday, holding one more chart in the same hand as his annotated set — and in its margin, a small, careful sketch of a bird. Evidence, not an answer: the hand that marks charts draws birds. Nix will do the rest himself.

Delivery: each resident's Day 3 brief carries only what their own orbit holds (plus the spool if their morning takes them past the fountain). The placements are facts of the dawn, unexplained, like the box.
