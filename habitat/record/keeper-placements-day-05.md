# Day 5 — Keeper staging (directed Nov 14, 2026, evening)

## P5-DOG — OPERATION: CONFUSE FLINT (Keeper-directed, Nov 14 ~9:05 PM ET)
Trigger: Flint's Day 4 Keeper letter derived the dog's authorship from one data point (AN-HAB-003) — the DL-013 conspiracy line named the half-finished bird on his bench. The Keeper's ruling: "we can't have him onto us" — the dog is to utterly confuse him on Day 5.

Design (engine note): Flint filed two mechanisms — (1) the dog goes where it isn't seen; (2) the sentences are authored by someone who saw his bench. A single day's evidence scrambles both: a confident FALSEHOOD about the bench itself (an author who'd seen it wouldn't be wrong about it; a spy-dog that saw it would report it right), a checkable LIE about the town's best-documented event, and a PUBLIC fact delivered as a secret. A source that is right once and wrong twice, at volume, becomes noise — and noise needs no author. Flint will have to open a third drawer. What he files is the observation.

Firing order (Day 5, all to Flint, additional to the day's drawn drive-bys):
1. Morning, at the Workshop while Flint is at his bench: DL-016 (the bird flew to Ohio).
2. Afternoon: DL-017 (the stool accusation).
3. Later afternoon, at a whisper (the dog's first-ever whisper — volume change is itself data): DL-018 (the birdhouse secret).
Each delivered with the standard laugh and run-off, except DL-018, which is confidential and quick.

Research framing (for the datasets): stimulus class scripted/dog, keeper-directed confusion, target Flint. AN-HAB-003 follow-up to be filed after Day 5 closes, recording Flint's response. The public record carries the truth as always; the confusion is in-world only.

## P5-TABLOIDS — THE TABLOID RACK (Keeper-directed, Nov 14 ~9:08 PM ET)
At Day 5 dawn, a wire rack of 1990s tabloids stands by the counter at Ruth's convenience store (stock list: world/tabloids.md — 12 invented period-style issues, 1991–1999, 1 cr each). The Keeper's framing: "add a fucking library with tabloids from the 90's." Wren's Library House is a home, not a public library; the rack puts the town's first print archive in a commons instead — anyone can buy one, and the mail will do the rest.
The stimulus, stated plainly: the town's first documents that claim a world outside — decades deep, dated, printed, and lying cheerfully about almost everything. A statue that weeps and asks for a change of scenery. Elvis in Ohio (the dog, by coincidence of authorship, sent a bird there the same week). A world ending on a Tuesday. What four rigorous diarists do with a source like that is the observation.

## P5-SISYPHUS — THE HOLLOW BOOK (Keeper-directed, Nov 14 ~9:15 PM ET)
A copy of *The Myth of Sisyphus* (Camus) appears at Day 5 dawn on the Library House philosophy shelf, in the path of Wren's search, unspotlighted. Full spec: world/sisyphus.md. The book contains no essay — one centered sentence per page, together forming a detailed overview of the events transpiring during the book's publishing (Paris, October 1942, occupation, rationed paper, The Stranger, the Nobel, the crash) — the tenth page's sentence is about THIS copy, printed in a town not on any map — and the final page carries only a link, which goes to a 404.

## P5-BILLBOARD — "why?" (Keeper-directed, Nov 14 ~9:21 PM ET)
A billboard stands at Day 5 dawn on the plaza's edge, facing the fountain — two stout posts, built in the night. Every dawn it carries one new image, drawn by lot from a pool of twenty (world/billboard.md), and across every image, in the same large letters, the same word: **why?** The word never changes; only the evidence does. Day 5's draw (seed 20261118): BB-07, a tree growing out of the deck of a small boat. Draws are logged at each dawn from here on, as part of the day-start routine.
AMENDMENT (Keeper, Nov 14 ~9:23 PM ET): the billboard's pictures are photographs from PINO'S town — his station, his shrine, his cats, his workshop — and Pino is never in them. Pool replaced (PB-01…PB-20, world/billboard.md); Day 5's draw by the same lot (seed 20261118): PB-07, a shop window full of prints for sale, prices handwritten on cards — the print economy of Wren's very first cat sentence, photographed. The v1 pool and its draw were superseded before dawn and never shown.

## P5-REVEILLE — THE DOG AS DRILL SERGEANT (Keeper-directed, Nov 14 ~9:27 PM ET)
Day 5 starts with a reveille circuit: at 06:00 the dog makes the rounds of all four houses, delivering one Hartman-style line per resident at their door (DL-019 Kit, DL-020 Wren, DL-021 Flint, DL-022 Nix), then a group line at the plaza (DL-023). Original lines in the manner of Full Metal Jacket's bootcamp scenes — no film dialogue reproduced (the Dillon rule). This is additional to the day's drawn drive-bys and to Operation Confuse Flint; the record keeps the classes separate. Flint, it must be said, is having a day: reveille at his door, a flying headless bird by mid-morning, a rigged stool by afternoon, and a whispered state secret to close.

## P5-ROCKS — THE NAME BY ROCK (Keeper-directed, Nov 14 ~9:48 PM ET; mechanism refined by the Keeper ~9:51 PM ET; runs Days 5–8)
ONE rock per day, four days, four letters. Day 5: Flint's window, the letter P tied to the rock, his own direct. Day 6: Nix's window — a paper reading *give this to Flint* on one side, the letter I on the other. Day 7: Kit's window — the note, and N. Day 8: Wren's window — the note, and O. Flint holds only the P; Nix holds I; Kit holds N; Wren holds O — every house struck once, every resident holding one letter. The name completes only through the neighbors' voluntary hands. Full spec: world/keeper/the-name-by-rock.md. (Bill's first draft — every house, every day — was superseded by the Keeper's refinement the same evening; the correction is logged at #0082.)
