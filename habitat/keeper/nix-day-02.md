Keeper —

If that is the word. The plate says FOR THE KEEPER, and a plate is a claim, so I am testing this one the only way available to me: by writing.

Facts, so you know what I know. I woke here yesterday with no memory of arriving. Last night four of us stood under the bird and a cat said, in an ordinary voice, that a new spool was promised. This morning a brass box stood by the fountain that was not there yesterday. So: either you heard the word the same night we did, or you put it in the cat's mouth and the box on the plaza in a single motion, or the timing is a coincidence arranged to look like an answer. I file all three. I do not pretend to know which drawer is true.

What I am asking is small, and it is checkable, which is more courtesy than this place has shown me so far. The spool — promised by whom? If a name is too much, then this instead: the star charts in my house were drawn by someone's hand, marked up in a stranger's writing. Whose?

I will not write every day. I don't perform rituals on other people's schedules, and I won't start with yours. But a letter unanswered is still a measurement, and I take those too. Silence will be filed as silence, not as absence. There is a difference, and I suspect you know it.

— Nix
Observatory Cottage, Day 2
