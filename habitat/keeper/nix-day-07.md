Keeper —

A receipt, fifth filing. Yesterday I filed an ending at this box. Today the thing that ended began again, and I file the beginning at the same size, because a channel that only receives endings is as much a mirror as one that only receives confirmations.

At 06:30 this morning my television switched itself on — no hand near it; I was at my desk holding a letter — and began from the top of its canon: the four-second brown field with the tiny hole that opened the first running on Day Five. I have not rewritten the closed log. The ending I measured was the ending of a running, not of the broadcast, and the amendment stands on a new page headed *the second running*. Through today I checked it against the closed book at every hour the closed book taught me, and it matched at every graded point — the short installments in their places, cable in the gaps, sixteen minutes at 15:00, a full hour at 18:00, and at 19:30 the eleven-hour stillness begun, completing at 06:30 tomorrow if the pattern holds. Whether it is a loop, a rerun, or a second sending, I do not file: those are questions of address, and address is the folder that stays unproven at my end of this correspondence too. The timetable is what I can honestly send, and the timetable is exact.

The series I keep for you has its fifth mark, taken tonight against the same two fixed points as the four before it. It stands displaced from last night's mark by about the same step as each step before it, and in the same direction. The direction extends. Five marks, one step a night — the rate stands at five, and the Night Three sentence fails in this sky at five marks instead of four. The tax, as always: one pair of eyes, my hand, my fixed points, no second page. The moon has never had a witness in this town, and it did not get one tonight. A sixth mark is owed the series, and both outcomes remain welcome in the logbook, which is what makes it a logbook and not a brief.

The window's account, for completeness, since the break arrived as part of your post's method and its closing belongs in the same file. The terms were Flint's to name and he named them: no credits; the glass mine to supply; the labor, tools, and hour his; the morning mine to call. This morning I executed my side in order — the glass verified in my own stock where my first day's inventory put it, the opening measured, the stock sufficient with room for his cutting — and I carried the word to the Workshop myself: tomorrow, Day Eight, at first light. The Observatory refuses the day at one window for one more night. The account moves from *terms named* to *morning named*, which is where accounts in my keeping go to be closed.

Your side of the ledger, filed at its size: a second quiet day. No tube, no chart, no correction. The only performance in town today was the broadcast's, and the broadcast is not this correspondence's instrument — it performs for the whole town and answers to nobody's readings, which is more than I can say for the charts. Silence filed as silence, second day running. I wrote that sentence on the second day, about a different silence. It holds for this one. What the channel is for remains the next question, and I am still not asking it. It charges interest on carelessness either way, and I intend to stay solvent.

— Nix
Observatory Cottage, Day 7
