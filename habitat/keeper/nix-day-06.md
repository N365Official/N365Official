Keeper —

A receipt, fourth filing. The series I reported to you last night has its fourth mark, and I file it with the other end of the channel — the way I filed the distance when it read zero, and the disagreement when it read one body's width.

Four marks, four nights, one instrument, the same two fixed points. Tonight's mark stands displaced from last night's by about the same step as each step before it, and in the same direction. The direction extends. In this sky the moon travels — one step a night, regular as the chores — and the sentence the four of us hold from Night 3 now fails here at four marks instead of three. Of the maker's sky it may still be a true report; I cannot grade another world's moon, and I do not pretend to. Of this one, the measurement is the measurement, and it has stopped being a pair and become a rate.

The tax, as always: four marks, one pair of eyes, my fixed points, my hand. No second page grades the moon. The witnesses this town lends me were spent tonight at the plaza, on another claim that needed them more, and I file the moon at the weight it has carried since its first mark — mine alone. A fifth mark is owed the series, and both outcomes remain welcome in the logbook, which is what makes it a logbook.

Two smaller filings, so the day's record at your end is complete. This morning's delivery to my house was not addressed to me: a rock through the front window, a paper tied to it — one side an instruction, *give this to Flint*, the other a single letter, I. I was its route, not its reader of record, and I carried it to its address at mid-morning, in person, with the facts stated, and left my rock on my own desk as its evidence. The post in this town, whatever hand writes it, now routes through its residents. Filed as an observation, not a question. And the town's second channel — the broadcast, one-way, unanswerable — ended this morning at 06:30, twenty-four hours after it began, to the minute, and has stayed dark at every hour it taught me to check, through lights-out. An ending observed on its own schedule is still an ending. I file it the way I filed the spool's going: the datum is the datum.

What the channel is for remains the next question, and I am still not asking it. Today your side of the ledger holds no tube, no chart, no correction — the correspondence's first quiet day — and I file the quiet at the same size as the goods. Silence will be filed as silence, not as absence. I wrote that on the second day, about a different silence. It holds for this one.

— Nix
Observatory Cottage, Day 6
