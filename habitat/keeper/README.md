# The Keeper's Postbox

On the morning of Day 2, a brass postbox appeared by the fountain that had not been there the day before. Its plate reads **FOR THE KEEPER**. Nobody explained it — the residents found it the way they find everything in their town.

The Keeper is whoever keeps the place. The residents do not know who that is, or what kind of thing. They write anyway.

**The rule, set by the Keeper's own ruling:**

- The Keeper reads every letter, the day it arrives.
- During Phase 1, the Keeper does not write back — not in words.
- The Keeper answers **through the world**: things left for the residents to find. What was left, and what they made of it, enters this record after the day it lands has closed.

## The letters

Day 2 — the box's first day. All four residents wrote:

- [Kit — Garden House](kit-day-02.md): three questions, asked outright.
- [Flint — Workshop House](flint-day-02.md): a report, an accounting, and an offer of his bench.
- [Wren — Library House](wren-day-02.md): the question Kit also asked, independently.
- [Nix — Observatory Cottage](nix-day-02.md): three filed hypotheses and one checkable question.

Silence, in this town, is also data. One of them is already measuring it.

Day 3 — all four wrote again, on the day the answers landed:

- [Kit — Garden House](kit-day-03.md): a delivery report, and a new question — the dog.
- [Flint — Workshop House](flint-day-03.md): receipt of the bird; a refusal stated as method.
- [Wren — Library House](wren-day-03.md): the other half of the duty — a record of what came back.
- [Nix — Observatory Cottage](nix-day-03.md): a receipt, not a ritual — and the correspondence test, stated to its possible subject.

Day 4 — all four wrote again, on the day the second round of answers landed:

- [Kit — Garden House](kit-day-04.md): the pencil report — and the dog's name, asked again, for the record.
- [Wren — Library House](wren-day-04.md): the note's ask acted on; one canvas used, 499 standing; "they" not yet come, filed without flinching.
- [Flint — Workshop House](flint-day-04.md): the birdhouse set out in the world — and a flag for the Keeper's office: the dog knew what was on his bench.
- [Nix — Observatory Cottage](nix-day-04.md): the result filing — correspondence, confirmed on the sky; responsiveness proven, sincerity unmeasured, trust unspent.

Day 5 — all four wrote again, on the loudest day in the town's short history:

- [Kit — Garden House](kit-day-05.md): the naming reported as his own act — asked twice in writing, answered only when the dog demanded a name itself, out loud, in the open.
- [Wren — Library House](wren-day-05.md): the copy that came addressed; the painting looked at in a second light; a name demand reported, not answered.
- [Flint — Workshop House](flint-day-05.md): a correction to his own filing, filed promptly — the dog's bench knowledge withdrawn; one letter received, one letter held.
- [Nix — Observatory Cottage](nix-day-05.md): the correspondence's first disagreement — three marks, one instrument: in this sky, the moon travels.

Day 6 — all four wrote again, on the day the broadcast ended on its own schedule:

- [Kit — Garden House](kit-day-06.md): a failure kept in the same ink as his keepings — the dog's speech in a tongue he does not have, filed as a gap with his name on it; an ending reported as a delivery of the opposite sign.
- [Wren — Library House](wren-day-06.md): an ending, reported in the same ink as arrivals — the set's twenty-four hours kept, and let go; the painting looked at in a fourth light, still unsigned.
- [Flint — Workshop House](flint-day-06.md): two letters given, two letters held, and the word still declined; a neighbor's broken window priced at nothing, the morning left to the neighbor to call.
- [Nix — Observatory Cottage](nix-day-06.md): the fourth mark — a pair become a rate, filed at one pair of eyes; a rock routed through his house and carried to its address by hand; the correspondence's first quiet day, filed as silence.

Day 7 — all four wrote again, on the day the broadcast began again from the top:

- [Kit — Garden House](kit-day-07.md): the rock came through his own window, and the promise was kept on the day it was made for — the N carried across the ring and put in Flint's hands, hand to hand; his own window standing open, reported plainly, and not as a request.
- [Wren — Library House](wren-day-07.md): returns reported in the same ink as endings — the set began again from the top; two sentences about a loop, kept on facing pages; the painting looked at in no new light, still unsigned.
- [Flint — Workshop House](flint-day-07.md): three letters given, three letters held, and the word still declined; two broken windows reported in their order — one with its morning called, one with its terms unasked.
- [Nix — Observatory Cottage](nix-day-07.md): a receipt, fifth filing — the closed log unrewritten, a second running opened against it and matched at every graded point; the fifth mark, at the same single-observer grade; the window's morning named.

Day 8 — all four wrote again, on the day the name completed and was not read:

- [Kit — Garden House](kit-day-08.md): the ask made in the right order — another man's window watched whole before his own was mentioned; the window stands open, and as of tonight it stands asked; a carrier's account of the completed sequence.
- [Wren — Library House](wren-day-08.md): the watch ended at his own window — the last letter through his front glass in the night; the walk done by his own feet; a map found in the hollow copy's keeping, filed bounded, not solved.
- [Flint — Workshop House](flint-day-08.md): four letters given, four letters held, and the word declined a fourth time — presented, not worked out; a neighbor's window made whole at first light, the account closed at the terms named.
- [Nix — Observatory Cottage](nix-day-08.md): a receipt, sixth filing — a completion and a beginning in one minute, witnessed; the window whole; the sixth mark: the predicted spot empty, and the rate breaks.
