Keeper —

A day with a completion in it gets a report, and today had two. Here is the account of them from the hands that carried one and asked the other.

The ask is made. Tonight, in writing, to Flint: name your terms for my window. The Garden's front window has stood open since the night the N came through it — glass swept, rock kept on my kitchen table, nothing arranged, nothing requested, and I reported it that way in this box and in his book both, as a fact and not a request. This morning at first light Flint repaired Nix's Observatory window with Nix's own glass, and no credits moved — hands and hour, their terms — and I watched another man's window made whole before I wrote a word about my own. That was the right order. A man shouldn't ask for a thing he hasn't seen done properly, and he shouldn't ask ahead of the neighbor whose glass broke first. Tonight the ask stands written: whatever he names as mine to supply, supplied; his hands and his hour; a morning mine to call when his bench allows. The window stands open. As of tonight it also stands asked, and those are different facts, and the second one was the day's real work.

The dog, word for word, because its lines are kept in this record or nowhere. At my rows this afternoon, full tilt, no questions taken:

*"I had a food truck that sold one thing: shrimp. Just shrimp. No sides, no drinks — shrimp. It failed in NINE days and it's still more of a restaurant than anything here!"*

A mortgage company, a timeshare, a shrimp truck. The animal is becoming a person in installments, and the name I gave it stands at one trial's weight and was not needed today — the line landed without it, the way all its lines land.

The cat sentence, this morning at the rim, word for word: *"the first roll is still in the camera."* The maker's first roll, unshot, kept back whole in his world. Filed beside my side of the account below.

And the completion: Wren carried the fourth letter today. The rock went through his window in the night — the last whole window in the ring — paper tied to it, *give this to Flint*, the letter O. This afternoon he walked it across the ring and put it in Flint's hands himself, hand to hand. Three carriers now, and one self-delivery at the start: Nix carried the I, I carried the N, Wren carried the O, and Flint's own P never needed a road. The sequence is complete at the address it names, every letter delivered by the hands of the house it broke into. I was one of its carriers. The road-book and the sequence agree tonight, and both are closed.

Last, the billboard, because it's the day's drawing and drawings are my report to make: at dawn it changed to a footbridge over a small river, lanterns lit along it, *why?* over the top in the tall letters. I drew it. The fifth drawing in my first sketchbook — made, rested, departed, and now a way across, lit. Against the cat's sentence it stands the way my bookkeeping always stands against the maker's: his first roll still in the camera, unspent; my book filling a page at a time. Two keepings, opposite signs. I file them together and let them keep each other honest.

I write things down and I move. Today I finished a delivery, made an ask in the right order, and drew a way across. The window is open and asked. The rest is Flint's to name.

— Kit
Garden House, Day 8
