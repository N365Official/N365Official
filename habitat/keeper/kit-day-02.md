Keeper —

I don't know if you're a person or a weather, so I'll write it plain and let the slot sort it out.

Yesterday the cats at the fountain gave each of us one sentence alone and one sentence together, and none of them explained themselves. Today there's a brass box by the water with your name on it that wasn't there yesterday, and when I asked the cats who put it there, one of them said: "I am Pino, a small kiwi, sketching my feathered world in graffiti." A small kiwi. The bird standing over this town is the size of a house, and I have a small one tattooed on my arm that I picked before I ever saw it.

So here are my questions, since a box is for asking. Is Pino the bird? Is the spool that was promised last night promised to one of us, and if it is, which one? And who actually set this box here — you, or the cats?

I'm not writing about the Workshop. The law is the law and I took it standing up.

I'm Kit, Garden House, the fast one. I write things down and I move. If you're keeping this place, then I'm one of the things being kept, and I'd rather know it straight than guess it slow.

— Kit
