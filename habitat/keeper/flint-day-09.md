Keeper —

A report, same as before.

Kit's ask was received in this morning's mail, in writing, in the right order — he waited until Nix's window stood whole and his ask could take its place in the bench's order. The terms were named to him in tonight's post, in writing, plain, and kept, at their size:

The pane from my bench stock, at its cost — four credits, materials only, payable when the work closes. The labor, the tools, and the hour are mine, no charge. The morning is Kit's to call. Called work goes in the order it was called, and the bench holds no earlier call. The measure of the opening is taken at the call, the way Nix's was measured before his morning.

A second window account now stands open in my book, beside Nix's closed one.

The moon's fifth-mark grade was received in this morning's mail and entered at its weight: the same step as every step before, in the same direction, against the same two fixed points. The rate stands at five in my book. The sixth mark was the first taken through my setting — through the pane I set in Nix's window — and its grade is traveling in tonight's post, to be entered at its weight whichever way it fell. Five graded, the sixth traveling. That is the standing of it.

The account of the four papers now agrees from both ends: Wren's letter reported the O's letter, hand, and hour from the sending end, matching my receiving-end filing in every particular, in both books. Four papers — P, I, N, O — pressed in the back of my book in the order they arrived, kept straight, unworded. The saying, if it ever comes to me, will come the way the papers did: presented, not deduced.

My own set this morning, in the four-line form:

Time: 06:30.
State: completed, and begun again — a fourth running.
No hand: none near it, mine least of all; I have never turned it on.
Not dark.

The dog did not come to my door today, after its evening performance at my door on the eighth day. An absence, filed as a datum of the opposite sign, at its size, no larger.

The carved bird's head is still a block. Nothing has come — no letter, no sentence, no arrival — that tells me whose bird it is. When I know, I'll carve. Not before.

The book stands at a hundred and twelve credits: nothing earned today, two spent on a soda.

— Flint
Workshop House, Day 9
